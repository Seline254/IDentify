📷 Photo tips

💡Use good lighting - avoid shadows covering the text
📐Lay the document flat and shoot directly above it
🔍All four corners should be visible in the frame
🚫Don't crop out the name or ID number - these are used for matching

🔒 Privacy & safety

The document photo is never shown publicly. Only the owner can view full details - and only after they pass face verification. You're helping, not exposing.
