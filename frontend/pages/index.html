ID IDentify

Student / Public Portal

Search by registration number and locate your ID immediately.

Students can check whether a found student ID is currently being held at a campus location. The portal only confirms the location of the item and does not replace the officer hand-over process.

Use your registration number to check whether your ID is currently held at a campus desk or security office.

Officer Portal

What to expect

  • Enter your registration number.
  • See whether the ID has been logged and where it is being held.
  • Visit the stated campus location to collect it in person.

For students

This portal is intentionally limited to registration-number searches. Students cannot upload documents, verify identity online, or manage inventory.

For officers

Authorized campus officers use the dedicated officer dashboard to log new IDs, track active inventory, and mark items as handed over once they are collected.

Students use this portal for quick lookup. Officers manage the intake and hand-over workflow from the dedicated dashboard.
